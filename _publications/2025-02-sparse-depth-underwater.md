---
title: "Sparse Depth Completion for Underwater Inspection Robots"
authors: "M. Bergstrom, L. Ferreira, A. Novak"
venue: "IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA)"
year: 2025
status: "accepted"
codeurl: "https://github.com/orbital-robotics-lab/uwnet-sparse-depth"
abstract: >-
  Underwater inspection platforms usually carry a single-beam sonar and a
  monocular camera. We combine both into a sparse-to-dense depth estimator
  that remains stable in turbid water and at grazing angles.
---

The method fuses sonar returns with monocular features in a U-Net-style architecture. Field trials on a dam inspection vehicle show consistent metric depth up to four metres.
