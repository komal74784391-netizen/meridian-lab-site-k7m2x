---
title: "Continual Extrinsic Calibration for Multi-LiDAR Rigs"
authors: "T. Ilić, S. Haruki, P. Adeyemi"
venue: "IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS)"
year: 2025
status: "accepted"
abstract: >-
  Extrinsic parameters of a multi-LiDAR rig drift as the vehicle flexes and
  sensors age. We formulate continual calibration as an online factor-graph
  problem that re-estimates transforms from overlapping returns without a
  calibration target.
---

The estimator runs on the vehicle's onboard computer and degrades gracefully when the overlap between sensors shrinks. Evaluation uses three rigs over six months of operation.
