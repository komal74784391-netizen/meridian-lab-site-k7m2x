year: 2024

codeurl: "https://github.com/komal74784391-netizen/adverse-weather-depth-k7m2x"
abstract: >-
  Existing outdoor depth benchmarks are dominated by clear-weather drives. We
  collect and annotate a multi-season dataset covering rain, fog, snow and low
  sun, and re-evaluate eight published estimators.
---

The benchmark includes a reproducible evaluation harness, per-weather splits and a leaderboard script. All baselines are re-trained with the same protocol so that comparisons are apples-to-apples.
