---
title: "Neuromorphic Optical Flow at the Edge"
authors: "W. Kobayashi, N. Haddad"
venue: "Workshop on Event-Based Vision (EBV)"
year: 2025
status: "accepted"
abstract: >-
  We study spiking optical-flow estimators that run within the power budget of
  a centimetre-scale drone. A recurrent spiking network matches dense-flow
  baselines on DSEC while using a fraction of the energy.
---

The workshop paper reports an ablation over spiking neuron models and a deployment on a neuromorphic edge processor. No model implementation has been published for this paper.
